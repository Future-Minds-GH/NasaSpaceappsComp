# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Use the live site. Back-up plan: the screen recording (see the end).
All data on the site is real. Scores in Absolute mode (the default) are often yellow or red for the Moon and Mars: Earth is never as cold at night. Say this before the first result, so a low score reads as a finding, not a failure.

| Time | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Show the landing page | "NASA needs places on Earth to test Moon and Mars hardware. The famous ones, like the Atacama and Devon Island, are well known. We find new ones, and we say *why* they match, with data." |
| 0:20 | Explain the idea | "We cut Earth, the Moon and Mars into equal-area tiles about 27 km across. Every tile gets the same nine factors: day and night temperature, day-night swing, sunlight, slope, roughness, elevation, surface type, minerals, ice. So one search works between any two bodies." |
| 0:40 | **Mars → Earth.** Click Jezero Crater. Compare with Earth | "Jezero is where Perseverance landed. These are the closest Earth tiles." Open the best match. "The score breaks down by factor: terrain matches, temperature does not." |
| 1:20 | Show "where the analogy breaks" on the result | "No Earth site matches Mars gravity, its thin air or its radiation." "We show this on every result." |
| 1:40 | **Moon → Earth.** Click an Artemis III site near the south pole | "This is a candidate Artemis III region. Here are the Earth places most like it." Move the preset or a slider. "The ranking updates live, in the browser." |
| 2:15 | **Mars → Mars.** Search from Jezero on Mars | "The same tool also scouts other landing sites on the same body." |
| 2:35 | Open About, "Does it work?" | "We checked it. For 5 of 5 Moon and Mars targets, a known Earth analog ranks in Earth's top 10%. 48 of our 50 top-10 matches are new places. And 20 of 20 places that should never match, such as Paris and the Amazon, score red." |
| 2:50 | Close | "All open NASA, ESA and JAXA data. A new mission's map becomes one more factor, with no retraining." |

## Rules

- Limits to say if asked: tolerances and weights are set by hand, not learned; a tile is about 27 km across, so a result is a region; Moon mineral scores are weak (no feldspar-rich class).

- Read only numbers that the site shows. Do not quote numbers from sample data.
- If the live search fails, the site falls back to `showcase.json` for the named sites. Click a named site.
